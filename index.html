Iran fires on Kuwait and Bahrain; US hits back at Qeshm

Iran attacked Ali Al Salem Air Base, a base used by US forces in Kuwait, and struck targets in Bahrain. The US responded with strikes on Iranian missile launch sites on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, where a fifth of the world's oil passes.
